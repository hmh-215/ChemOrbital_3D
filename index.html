ChemOrbital 3D — Controls & Shortcuts Guide

Interaction Modes & Hotkeys

1 / D
🧪 Demos Mode: Explore textbook organic, inorganic, and orbital gallery models with free 3D rotation.
2 / B
🔨 Build Mode: Click orbital lobes to attach atoms with Quick Build, use manual build, and create covalent σ or π/δ bonds. Hold & drag mouse to box-select multiple atoms.
3 / C
⚙️ Configure Mode: Select 1 or multiple atoms to customize properties (charge, radius, rotation with bond snap points, display orbital, styling, outlines, nodals).
4 / M
📊 Molecular Orbital (MO) Panel: Toggle quantum energy diagram, electron configuration & bond order.

3D Viewport & Camera Navigation

↑ ↓ ← →
Arrow Keys: Pan and shift the 3D camera smoothly in real time.
Space / R
Center Camera: Reset 3D camera view to origin.
🧭 Orientation Gizmo
3D Rotation Axes: Drag gizmo to rotate view; click any axis node (+X, −X, +Y, −Y, +Z, −Z) to snap camera into orthogonal view.
Left Drag
Orbit camera in Demos mode, or box-select multiple atoms in Build / Configure mode.
Right Drag
Pan camera across the 3D viewport.
Mouse Wheel
Zoom in / zoom out.

Atom Selection & Editing

Left Click
Select atom or click orbital lobe to attach with Quick Build.
Hold & Drag
Draw marquee box to select multiple atoms (Build & Configure modes).
Ctrl + Click
Toggle atom into/out of current multi-selection.
Shift + Double Click
Select all atoms of the same element (e.g. all Hydrogens).
Double Click
Focus and zoom camera directly onto selected atom.
Ctrl + Z
Undo last change (supports up to 3 reversals).
Ctrl + Y
Redo change.
Delete / Backspace
Delete selected atom(s).
